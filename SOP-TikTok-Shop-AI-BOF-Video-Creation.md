## Method 1 — Women's Wear: Pinterest Motion Copy

**Best for:** Fashion, clothing, women's wear products  
**Result:** AI avatar (Pinterest model) replicates the exact movement of a proven viral video

### Tools
- **Sales Spy** — find viral BOF videos in Women's Wear
- **Snaptic** — remove watermark and download the video
- **Pinterest** — find a female model whose motion you'll copy
- **Higgsfield Motion Control** — AI sync

### Steps

1. **Find a viral BOF video (Sales Spy)**
   - Filter by **Women's Wear**
   - Look for BOF (Bottom of Funnel) content — videos driving direct purchase
   - Find one you like the look of
   - Click the redirect button and copy the link

2. **Download watermark-free (Snaptic)**
   - Go to Snaptic
   - Paste the TikTok link
   - Download the clean video

3. **Find your Pinterest model**
   - Go to Pinterest
   - Find a woman whose movement and energy matches the vibe of the product/video
   - This person becomes your AI avatar

4. **Generate the AI video (Higgsfield → Motion Control)**
   - Left column: upload the Sales Spy viral video (source motion)
   - Right column: upload your Pinterest model (avatar)
   - Settings: **Kling Motion Control, 1080p**
   - Generate

5. **Post** with TikTok Shop affiliate product link

**Output:** The Pinterest model appears to be doing exactly what the person in the original viral video was doing. Indistinguishable from real UGC.

## Method 2 — Any Product: AI UGC Recreation

**Best for:** Home goods, gadgets, lifestyle products with a visual transformation/change  
**Result:** Hyper-realistic AI UGC video recreating a viral product in action

### Tools
- **Sales Spy** — find trending products with upward GMV
- **Higgsfield** — generate the AI UGC video

### Steps

1. **Find a trending product (Sales Spy)**
   - Go to Sales Spy → **Products tab**
   - Filter by **past 30 days**
   - Look for products with a **visual change** (before/after, transformation, cleaning, steam, etc.)
   - Check the GMV chart — look for a **strong upward trend**
   - Example: A steam cleaner "doing absolute numbers" on an upward GMV chart

2. **Generate the AI video (Higgsfield)**
   - Use the product image/footage as your reference
   - Generate a hyper-realistic AI UGC video showing the product in use
   - Focus on the visual change/transformation the product creates

3. **Post** with TikTok Shop affiliate product link

**Output:** A realistic AI UGC video that looks like a genuine product review/demo, no Pinterest model required.
