## Overview

A fully automated, faceless TikTok Shop affiliate system using AI-generated videos. No face, no voice, no filming, no editing. Products are promoted via AI-generated videos with AI voiceover. Commissions (~20%) are earned automatically when viewers purchase through the in-video link.

**Revenue potential:** $1,000–$2,300+/day at scale. Can be outsourced entirely to a VA.

**Key mechanism — GMV Max / Ad Spend Hijacking:** Brands give ad budget to TikTok's AI algorithm (not individual creators). By optimizing for conversion, your videos get chosen to receive brand ad spend automatically — meaning the brand runs paid ads on your video at zero cost to you.

## Phase 2 — Product Research (10–20 Min/Day)

**Product selection is everything.** Wrong product = no money. Good product = commissions flow automatically.

### Method 1 — New Releases on Big Brands (Inside TikTok App)
1. Open TikTok Shop inside the app
2. Find a major brand (e.g., Nell, Shark, Ninja)
3. Sort products by **"New Releases"**
4. Pick recently launched products — low competition, brand still has full ad budget
5. Grab the product listing image for video creation

### Method 2 — Copy Winning Creators
1. Find creators already making money on TikTok Shop
2. Check what products they're promoting
3. Make AI videos for the same product

### Method 3 — Explode Method via Caldata.com ⭐ PRIMARY METHOD

**The 200 Method (invented by the community):**

1. Go to [caldata.com](https://caldata.com) (~$30–$100/month)
2. Navigate to **Products → Yesterday**
3. Set filters:
   - **Revenue Growth Rate (minimum):** 200
   - **Creator Number (maximum):** 200
4. Click Submit
5. Results = products whose revenue at least doubled yesterday AND have fewer than 200 creators on them

**What to look for in results:**
- Revenue growth rate of 200%+ (the higher the better)
- Creator count under 200 (not saturated)
- **Ads icon (purple) on every video** — this confirms the brand has active ad spend running on creator videos

**Example:** A floor scrubber doing $50,000 revenue yesterday with only 80 creators = perfect product.

**Method via Brand Pages on Caldata:**
1. Go to **Shop** tab on Caldata
2. Find major brands (QVC, Dr. Max, etc. — $10M+ in monthly sales)
3. Open the brand, filter by **Last 7 Days**, go to back pages (less saturated)
4. Look for products with fewer than 20–30 creators AND purple ad icons on every video
5. These products = brand ad spend available, low competition

**Daily goal:** Find 10–15 products.

## Phase 3 — Video Creation (5 Min Per Video)

### Tools Required
- **Cling AI** — [kling.ai](https://kling.ai) (~$20/week) — primary video generator
- **ChatGPT** — free — script generation
- **ElevenLabs** — free tier works — text-to-speech voiceover
- **TikTok app** — final assembly

### Step 1 — Generate the AI Video (Cling AI)
1. Get the product image from Caldata or the TikTok product listing
2. Go to Cling AI → **Generate → Video Generation**
3. Upload the product image
4. Enter this prompt template:
   ```
   Put this product on a nice kitchen island and panel zoom on it
   ```
   *(Adjust location to match product: vacuum = living room, skincare = bathroom)*
5. Settings:
   - Resolution: **1080p**
   - Duration: **10 seconds**
   - Outputs: **1**
6. Generate — costs ~100 credits (~$0.50)

### Step 2 — Write the Script (ChatGPT)
Use this exact prompt:
```
Give me a 15-second script for a TikTok Shop bottom-of-funnel video selling the sale and creating urgency.
```

**Example of a good output:**
> "If this is still in your cart, stop scrolling. It's 30% off right now and TikTok is stacking a coupon on top of it. Thousands already grabbed it this week. Once it sells out, the price goes back up. Tap the link, check out before midnight. Lock in the discount. Don't come back tomorrow paying full price."

If the script is weak, just regenerate. Good scripts are the norm.

### Step 3 — Generate the Voiceover (ElevenLabs)
1. Go to [elevenlabs.io](https://elevenlabs.io)
2. Paste the script
3. Settings:
   - **Speed:** All the way up
   - **Exaggeration:** All the way up
   - Stability and similarity: default is fine
4. Choose any voice (male or female — test to find what converts)
5. Download the audio

### Step 4 — Assemble the Video (TikTok App)
1. Open TikTok, create new video
2. Import the AI-generated video from Cling
3. Add the ElevenLabs voiceover as the audio
4. Add on-screen text matching the script
5. Adjust background music if desired
6. Post with the affiliate product link attached

**Final video should be:** ~15 seconds, product shown, voiceover running, text reinforcing urgency, affiliate link in the video.

## Key Rules for Success

1. **Product selection is everything** — bad product = no results regardless of video quality
2. **Always verify ads are running** (purple icon in Caldata) before picking a product
3. **New + low creator count** = opportunity (use the 200 Method daily)
4. **Post volume wins** — 10–20 videos/day, not 1–2
5. **Be early** — AI lets you post without having the product, so you can jump on trending products immediately
6. **Don't over-optimize early** — get to $100–200/day first, then refine scripts and prompts
