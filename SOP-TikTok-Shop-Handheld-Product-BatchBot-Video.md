## Step 1 — Product Research on SalesSpy

1. Go to **https://www.salespy.org** → log in with Google (jayceeai24@gmail.com)
2. Click the **Products** tab
3. Set **Time Period** filter to **Last 30 Days**
4. Set **Region** to **United States**
5. Browse the results — look for small handheld products with:
   - High **GMV** (Gross Merchandise Value) — ideally $50K+
   - Strong **Weekly Growth %** — look for 500%+ as a signal of momentum
   - High **Total Units Sold**
6. Note the top candidates — record product name, GMV, and growth rate

> **VA: Open https://www.salespy.org in your browser. Click "Sign in with Google" and choose the jayceeai24@gmail.com account. Once logged in, click the "Products" tab at the top. On the left side you will see filters — set Time Period to "Last 30 Days." Scroll through the products listed. You are looking for small products that can be held in one hand (like a handheld steam cleaner, a facial tool, a portable charger, etc.). Write down the top 3–5 products that have the highest GMV number and the highest Weekly Growth % shown on their card.**

**What makes a good handheld product:**
- Physically small enough to hold in one hand
- Has a clear visual use-case (cleaning, beauty, health, grooming, gadget)
- Strong upward GMV trend — not flat or declining
- Not already saturated (check creator count if available)

## Step 4 — Download the Product Image

1. On SalesSpy, click the **"Open in TikTok Shop"** button on the product card
2. This opens the product page on TikTok Shop
3. Browse through the product images at the top of the page
4. Find the cleanest image — ideally just the product by itself, no extra accessories or attachments spread around it
5. Right-click on that image → **Save image as** → save it to your computer

> **VA: On SalesSpy, find the product card and click the icon that says "Open in TikTok Shop" — this will open the TikTok Shop product page in a new tab. On that page, look at the photos at the top. You want to find a photo that shows just the main product cleanly — not a photo with 12 accessories spread all around it, and not a lifestyle photo with a person in it. Right-click on the best photo and choose "Save image as" and save it to your Desktop or Downloads folder. Name the file something simple like "product-name.jpg".**

> **VA NOTE — What if there's no clean photo?** If every photo shows the product surrounded by accessories or in a lifestyle scene, just save the main/first product photo. BatchBot will remove the background automatically, so don't worry about the background color. Just make sure the product itself is clearly visible and takes up most of the photo.

## Step 5 — Upload to BatchBot and Generate AI Video

### Login

> **VA: Open https://batchbot.io in your browser. Click "Sign In" and enter: Email: jayceeai24@gmail.com | Password: QWerty132@!**

### Upload the Product Image

1. Click **📦 Products** in the left sidebar
2. Click the **Product Image** button
3. A file picker window will open — find and select the image you saved in Step 4
4. Type the product name in the **Product Name** field
5. Click **Add Product**
6. Wait a few seconds — you will see the message: *"Product uploaded with background removed."*

> **VA: After logging in, look at the left side of the screen and click "📦 Products." You will see a section at the top that says "Product Image" and "Product Name." Click the "Product Image" button — this opens a normal file picker window (like when you attach a file to an email). Find the product photo you saved in Step 4 and click Open. Then click in the "Product Name" box and type the product name. Then click the "Add Product" button. Wait a few seconds and you will see a green message that says the product was uploaded successfully. You should now see your product listed below.**

### Generate the AI Video

1. Click **✨ Create** in the left sidebar
2. Click **Start** on the **"Intro Video + AI"** template
3. Scroll down to the **"Generate New Footage"** section
4. Click **"Choose Products"**
5. Click on your product to select it (you will see a checkmark appear)
6. Click **"Done"**
7. Click your chosen **Scene Setting** button (from your decision in Step 3)
8. Click the **"11s — 300 credits/video"** duration button
9. Click **"Generate 1 Video"**
10. Wait 3–5 minutes — do not close the browser tab
11. When finished you will see: *"[Product Name] Saved to Library"*

> **VA: Click "✨ Create" on the left sidebar. Click the "Start" button under "Intro Video + AI." On the next screen, scroll down until you see the "Generate New Footage" section. If none of the presets fit, click **"Custom Prompt"** and type a short scene description in the text box. Keep it simple:

> *"The [product name] sits on a black marble kitchen counter next to a small potted plant. Soft lighting. The camera slowly moves around the product. No people."*

> **VA: If you need to use Custom Prompt, click that button and type your description in the box that appears. Keep it short — 2–3 sentences is enough. ## Key Notes

- **The AI is creative, not a robot** — it will put the product in the general scene you choose but may not follow very specific instructions (like "place it on a tree stump"). Stick to the preset scenes for best results.
- **Always use 11s** — it costs more credits but gives the best quality footage for TikTok.
- **One video per product slot** — if you generate again for the same product, it will overwrite the previous video. Always download before regenerating.
- **Credit balance** — check your remaining credits in the Subscription section before starting a batch of videos.
