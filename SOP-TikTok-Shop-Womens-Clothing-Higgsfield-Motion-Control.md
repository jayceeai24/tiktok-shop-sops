## Overview

This SOP covers finding a top-performing women's clothing product on SalesSpy and Kalodata (US region, last 30 days), downloading a viral video of a woman wearing the item, generating an AI character image using **Nana Banana Pro**, and then applying the viral video's motion to that character using **Kling Motion Control** inside Higgsfield.

**The final output is two clips edited together:**
1. An AI-generated hook clip (e.g. a celebrity or character doing something eye-catching)
2. The motion control clip — your AI character wearing the clothing product, mimicking the exact movements from the viral TikTok

**Output:** A hyper-realistic AI UGC video of a woman wearing the clothing product, indistinguishable from real creator content.

## STEP 1 — Product Research on SalesSpy (US, Last 30 Days, Womenswear)

1. Go to **https://www.salespy.org** → log in with Google (jayceeai24@gmail.com)
2. Click the **Products** tab
3. Click the **region selector** (top right of the page, may show "UK" by default) → select **US**
4. Click the **Time Period** dropdown → select **Last 30 days**
5. Click the **Product Category** dropdown → select **Womenswear & Underwear**
6. Browse the results — look for products with:
   - High **GMV** (Product GMV Trend amount — ideally $50K+)
   - Strong **Weekly Growth %** — look for 500%+ as a signal of momentum
   - High **Total Units Sold**
7. Write down your top 3–5 candidates

You are looking for items like bras, leggings, bodysuits, dresses, shapewear — anything women wear. Write down the names of the top 3–5 products with the highest GMV number and highest Weekly Growth % shown on their card.**

**What makes a good women's clothing product for this method:**
- It is worn ON the body (not a handbag, jewelry, or accessory)
- The product is clearly visible when a woman is wearing it
- It is simple enough that an AI avatar can replicate the look
- It has at least one viral video where a real woman is shown wearing it

## STEP 3 — Choose the Final Product and Find the Viral Video

1. Pick the ONE product with the best GMV + growth combination
2. On SalesSpy, click on the product name to open the product detail page
3. You will see **"Associated Videos"** — a list of viral TikTok videos for this product
4. Find a video that shows:
   - **A real woman wearing the clothing item** (not just holding it or reviewing it flat)
   - The woman is **clearly visible from at least waist up**
   - The video is **not a duet, split screen, or slideshow** — it should be a single continuous clip
   - Ideally the **#1 or #2 video** (highest view gain)
5. Click **"Open in TikTok Shop"** on that video to open the actual TikTok page
6. **Copy the full URL** from your browser address bar — it will look like:
   `https://www.tiktok.com/@username/video/1234567890123456789`

> **VA: On SalesSpy, click on the product name. Scroll down to the "Associated Videos" section. Look through the video thumbnails and find one where you can clearly see a woman wearing the clothing item. The best ones will show a before/after posture change, a try-on, or a woman showing off the item. Avoid videos that are just text on screen or show only the product lying flat. Click "Open in TikTok Shop" on the video you want. A new tab will open showing the TikTok video. Copy the full web address from the browser address bar — it should look like https://www.tiktok.com/@somename/video/some-long-number. Write it down.**

> **VA NOTE — What if the #1 video doesn't show a woman wearing the item?** Try the #2 or #3 video. What you need is a clear clip of a woman in motion wearing the product. If none of the top 10 videos show this, the product may not be right for this method — message your supervisor.

## STEP 5 — Get Your Character Image (Two Methods — Pick One)

There are two ways to get the character image that will be used in Motion Control. **Method A (Nana Banana Pro) is recommended** as it produces a more consistent, realistic result. Method B (Pinterest) is a faster fallback.

---

### METHOD A — Nana Banana Pro (Recommended)

1. Go to **https://nanabanana.pro** and log in
2. Upload **your photo** (or any woman's photo you want to use as the base character) in the environment/background you want
3. Upload the **character's photo** — this is the look/style you want the character to have
4. Add a simple prompt — you don't need anything specific, just enough to describe the character generating in the scene
5. Click Generate
6. Download the resulting character image — this is your avatar for Step 6

> **VA: Go to nanabanana.pro and log in. You need two photos: (1) a photo of yourself or the person who will be the "base" character, and (2) a photo of the character style/look you want. Upload both and add a short description prompt. Click Generate. When done, download the character image — this replaces the Pinterest photo in Step 6 below.**

> **Source:** Tutorial by @barchevskaia.socials — https://youtube.com/shorts/vaj8K_FmH-0

---

### METHOD B — Find Your Pinterest Model Avatar

1. Go to **https://www.pinterest.com** — use this if Nana Banana Pro is unavailable or you want a faster result
2. Search for something like: `women fitness model athletic wear` or `women yoga posture` or `women lifestyle portrait`
3. Browse the results and find a **photo of a woman** that matches the energy of the viral video:
   - Similar body type and posture to the woman in the viral video
   - Clear full-body or upper-body shot
   - Clean background preferred
   - Natural, non-filtered look works better for AI generation
   - **No group photos** — must be a single woman clearly visible
4. Click the photo to open it full size
5. Right-click the image → **Save image as** → save to your Desktop

> **VA: Open pinterest.com in your browser. In the search bar at the top, type "women athletic wear model" and press Enter. You will see a grid of images. You are looking for a photo of a single woman who looks similar to the woman in the TikTok video you downloaded — similar energy, body type, and vibe. The photo should show her face and body clearly, not be a group photo, and not be a heavily filtered or illustrated image. When you find a good one, click on it to open the full-size image, then right-click on it and choose "Save image as" to save it to your computer. Name it pinterest-model.jpg.**

**What makes a good Pinterest model for Higgsfield Motion Control:**
- Single woman, clearly visible face and body
- Upper body or full body visible — not just a face shot
- Athletic or casual/lifestyle style (matches clothing context)
- Clean, well-lit photo
- Similar movement energy to the viral video (if the viral video shows confident standing, pick a photo of a woman standing confidently)

- **Model:** Should default to **Kling 3.0 Motion Control** — leave it
- **Quality:** Leave at **720p** (sufficient for TikTok)
- **Scene control mode:** Leave the switch ON
